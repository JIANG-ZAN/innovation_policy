# 角色总设定图 v1 生成记录

## 生成方式

使用内置图像生成工具创建，作为网页游戏和动态PPT播片的角色视觉母版。

## 最终提示词

Use case: illustration-story

Asset type: master character design sheet for a branching interactive web game and animated PowerPoint cutscenes

Primary request: Create one polished, coherent character-bible illustration for the Chinese near-future life-story game “余生协议”. The image must establish the consistent visual identities of the default female protagonist 林一 across seven life stages, her mother 周岚, her lifelong friend 小满, and the non-humanoid personal AI 栖.

Scene/backdrop: clean warm ivory studio character-sheet background with subtle pale blue technical guide lines and restrained circular UI motifs, no environmental scene.

Subject:

1. Main row, visually dominant: the same Chinese woman 林一 shown at seven clearly readable life stages from left to right—newborn infant wrapped in a simple hospital swaddle; age 8 curious child with a neat short bob and practical school clothing; age 16 thoughtful teenager with tied-back dark hair; age 23 young adult in understated near-future clothing; age 38 capable professional with calm alert posture; age 58 mature woman with a few silver strands; age 98 silver-haired elder whose advanced healthcare makes her look physically around 70. Preserve unmistakably consistent facial lineage across every age: oval face, straight dark brows, warm brown almond eyes, small beauty mark below the left eye, calm observant expression. Clothing evolves from soft cream and muted teal into restrained charcoal-blue near-future garments; practical, ordinary-person design, not luxury fashion.
2. Smaller supporting reference portraits along the lower band: 周岚 at age 35 and age 93, recognizable as 林一’s mother through family resemblance but with a broader smile and stronger jaw; 小满 at age 8, 16, and late adulthood, energetic, slightly unruly wavy dark hair, expressive eyes, warmer rust-colored clothing, visually more organic and less system-conforming.
3. AI 栖 visual system: a non-humanoid hovering motif of concentric translucent rings around a small blue-white point of light, shown in three states—assistant, warning, and silent/waiting. It must not have a face or robot body.

Style/medium: premium cinematic 2D concept art and animation character-design sheet, elegant editorial science-fiction illustration, softly hand-painted with clean silhouettes and subtle texture; production-ready for a serious university interactive project.

Composition/framing: wide 16:9 sheet, all full figures visible with generous spacing, main seven-age lineup across the upper two-thirds, supporting portraits and AI motifs in a clean lower strip. Consistent scale progression and neutral front or three-quarter poses.

Lighting/mood: soft diffuse studio light, humane, intelligent, warm but faintly unsettling; hopeful near future rather than dystopian cyberpunk.

Color palette: warm ivory, muted teal, pale medical blue, charcoal, small rust accents; controlled low saturation.

Constraints: no written labels, no typography, no logos, no watermark; anatomically correct hands and faces; no duplicated limbs; no glowing neural implant permanently attached to 林一 because chip status is a player choice; keep every character distinct and consistent; support later extraction into separate layers.

Avoid: photorealism, anime style, chibi proportions, neon cyberpunk, battle armor, flashy hologram clutter, sexualized clothing, corporate logos, illegible text, random accessories.

## 后续使用

- 林一七个年龄版本应继续沿用椭圆脸、直眉、暖棕色杏眼和左眼下方小痣。
- 周岚沿用更明显的下颌线和温暖笑容。
- 小满沿用略显凌乱的卷发、铁锈色服装和更外向的神态。
- “栖”始终保持非人形环状界面，不生成机器人身体或人脸。
