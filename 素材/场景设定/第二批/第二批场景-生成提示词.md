# 第二批核心场景生成记录

## 统一规范

- 生成方式：Codex 内置 `image_gen`。
- 用途：Next.js 互动叙事游戏的 16:9 场景背景。
- 画面风格：电影感、半写实、近未来建筑概念设计；延续第一批象牙白、低饱和蓝灰与少量金色点缀。
- 构图要求：场景主体位于左侧与中部，右侧为人物立绘保留空间，底部为对话框保留相对平静的区域。
- 共通限制：不出现人物、可读文字、标志、按钮或水印；显示面板只使用抽象线条；避免霓虹赛博朋克、飞船感和过度复杂的全息界面。

## 05 人生规划中心

> Use case: stylized-concept  
> Asset type: 16:9 background environment for a narrative Next.js interactive life game  
> Primary request: Create “Hailan City Life Planning Center” in the year 2099, a calm futuristic civic interior that feels like a quiet airport terminal where young adults wait for algorithmic assignments of career, housing, relationships, and migration.  
> Scene/backdrop: a spacious curved hall overlooking a hazy coastal future city, sculptural ivory architecture, quiet rows of waiting seats and three understated gate-like consultation alcoves, a long blank translucent timeline ribbon floating in the middle-left with simple abstract nodes only.  
> Style/medium: cinematic semi-realistic architectural concept art, polished near-future 3D visualization, consistent with an elegant Chinese coastal city, believable rather than sci-fi spectacle.  
> Composition/framing: wide eye-level establishing shot, architectural features and timeline mostly on left and center, generous clean negative space in the right third for a character sprite, lower quarter visually calm for dialogue UI.  
> Lighting/mood: soft late-afternoon daylight, contemplative, orderly, slightly uncanny.  
> Color palette: warm ivory, misty blue-gray, pale cyan interface glow, very restrained rust-gold accents.  
> Constraints: no people, no readable text, no logos, no signs, no UI buttons, no watermark; any screens must be blank or contain only abstract lines; avoid neon cyberpunk, clutter, spaceship aesthetics, excessive holograms.

## 06 自动化工作与公共服务大厅

> Use case: stylized-concept  
> Asset type: 16:9 background environment for a narrative Next.js interactive life game  
> Primary request: Create the automated workplace and public service hall in Hailan City in the year 2114, the setting for a person’s final full workday after AI can perform most jobs and basic living services become universal.  
> Scene/backdrop: a flexible civic workplace combining an automated operations area and a public-service cooperation area: quiet robotic workstations and inactive desks on the left, a restrained circular human deliberation table in the center, modular service counters and a distant coastal city view; the room should imply that machines continue working while human purpose is uncertain.  
> Style/medium: cinematic semi-realistic architectural concept art, polished near-future 3D visualization, humane and believable, visually consistent with elegant ivory-and-blue environments.  
> Composition/framing: wide eye-level shot, visual activity on left and center, open uncluttered right third for character sprites, calm lower quarter for dialogue UI, strong depth without a central character.  
> Lighting/mood: clear morning light with a faint melancholy, efficient but not dystopian.  
> Color palette: ivory, warm gray, desaturated teal, pale cyan indicator lights, a few rust-gold structural accents.  
> Constraints: no people, no readable text, no logos, no signs, no UI buttons, no watermark; displays blank or abstract; no humanoid robots in foreground; avoid factory grime, cyberpunk neon, excessive machinery, spaceship look.

## 07 医疗决策室

> Use case: stylized-concept  
> Asset type: 16:9 background environment for a narrative Next.js interactive life game  
> Primary request: Create Anlan Hospital medical decision room in the year 2134, where an AI has denied an artificial-organ treatment and a family must decide whether to accept, appeal, or bypass the ruling.  
> Scene/backdrop: a dignified near-future hospital consultation room rather than an operating theater; one empty recovery bed or reclining medical chair on the left behind a subtle glass partition, a large blank translucent decision display in the center-left with abstract probability lines and one restrained red status light, a human review table, corridor light in the distance.  
> Style/medium: cinematic semi-realistic architectural concept art, polished near-future 3D visualization, emotionally grounded, consistent with elegant Chinese coastal-city interiors.  
> Composition/framing: wide eye-level shot, medical equipment and decision display left/center, generous right-side negative space for two character sprites, lower quarter clean for dialogue UI.  
> Lighting/mood: cool subdued daylight, solemn ethical tension, compassionate and clinical, not horror.  
> Color palette: cool gray-blue, ivory, desaturated teal, pale cyan, one small muted red warning accent.  
> Constraints: no people, no blood, no readable text, no logos, no signs, no UI buttons, no watermark; blank screens or abstract charts only; avoid prison-like architecture, harsh darkness, cyberpunk neon, excessive medical machinery.

## 08 长寿医学中心病房

> Use case: stylized-concept  
> Asset type: 16:9 background environment for a narrative Next.js interactive life game  
> Primary request: Create a private room in Anlan Hospital Longevity Medicine Center in the year 2174, where a 98-year-old person must choose between indefinite AI-integrated life extension, limited life extension with memory boundaries, or ending treatment and deleting personal data.  
> Scene/backdrop: a quiet windowless single patient room with an elegant adjustable bed on the left, a softly illuminated circular longevity-support apparatus above it, a simple bedside surface, and one open doorway at the back center leading to a calm corridor where distant human and service-robot silhouettes could pass but no figures are present; subtle data conduits disappear into the walls.  
> Style/medium: cinematic semi-realistic architectural concept art, polished near-future 3D visualization, humane, minimal, believable, visually consistent with the earlier ivory-and-blue hospital environments.  
> Composition/framing: wide eye-level shot, bed and apparatus left, doorway near center, generous uncluttered right third for an elderly character sprite or visitor, lower quarter calm and slightly darker for dialogue UI.  
> Lighting/mood: soft indirect evening light with no visible window, intimate, quiet, final, neither utopian nor grim.  
> Color palette: aged ivory, soft blue-gray, desaturated teal, faint warm gold, very restrained cyan glow.  
> Constraints: no people, no readable text, no logos, no signs, no UI buttons, no watermark; no three-choice panels baked into the image; avoid funeral imagery, horror, cyberpunk neon, clutter, spaceship aesthetics.
